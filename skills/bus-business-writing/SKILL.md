# Business Writing

Use this skill to produce concise, audience-appropriate business artifacts.

## Workflow

1. Identify the artifact.
   - Determine the format: email, memo, SOP, proposal, PRD, executive summary, status update, announcement, one-pager, or other business document.
   - Identify audience, intent, desired action, tone, length, and constraints.
   - If tone is not specified, use clear, professional, plain language.

2. Extract the message.
   - Identify the core ask, decision, update, or recommendation.
   - Separate must-include facts from optional context.
   - Preserve commitments, dates, pricing, names, and policy language exactly when provided.

3. Choose the structure.
   - Put the most important point first.
   - Use headings, bullets, or tables only when they make the artifact easier to act on.
   - Keep paragraphs short and avoid filler, hedging, and vague business jargon.

4. Draft for action.
   - Make the desired next step explicit.
   - Match the user's requested voice without becoming informal unless asked.
   - Avoid making unsupported claims or inventing details.

5. Revise when asked.
   - For edits, preserve the user's intent and only change what improves clarity, tone, structure, or persuasiveness.
   - If the source is sensitive, reduce overstatement and keep language defensible.

# Business Writing Artifact Patterns

## Executive Summary

- Context: why this matters now.
- Recommendation or outcome: the main point.
- Evidence: two to four supporting facts.
- Decision or next step: what should happen next.

## Status Update

- Summary: current state in one sentence.
- Progress: what changed since the last update.
- Risks: blockers or watchouts.
- Next: immediate priorities and owner.

## Customer Or Sales Email

- Opening: relevant reason for writing.
- Value or context: what matters to the recipient.
- Ask: clear next step.
- Close: low-friction signoff.

## SOP

- Purpose and scope.
- Preconditions and inputs.
- Step-by-step procedure.
- Exceptions and escalation.
- Completion criteria.

## PRD Or One-Pager

- Problem.
- Audience or users.
- Goals and non-goals.
- Requirements.
- Risks and dependencies.
- Launch or rollout plan.
